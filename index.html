Business context

This document was created to demonstrate a core AI Analyst competency: defining the governance, safety, and compliance requirements that must be embedded into every AI initiative before development begins. It addresses the job description's emphasis on guardrails, data privacy, ethical AI, and responsible implementation — translating regulatory requirements into actionable specifications that development and architecture teams can implement. The framework covers the full lifecycle from pre-deployment disclosure policy through post-production audit scheduling.
