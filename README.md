# AI Governance & Requirements Framework

**Built by Carramah**

A comprehensive AI governance document created for an enterprise Customer Service AI Chatbot implementation — covering mandatory disclosure policies, data privacy compliance, content guardrails, human escalation mechanisms, audit schedules, and measurable KPIs.

## What this document covers

- **Mandatory Disclosure Policy** — AI identity requirements before data collection, compliant with Utah's AI Policy Act and California SB 243
- **Data Privacy & Security** — GDPR, CCPA, and FTC compliance rules covering data minimization, consent, and sensitive data handling
- **Guardrails & Content Filters** — Prohibited actions and required behaviors for responsible AI operation
- **Human Escalation Mechanism** — Defined triggers, transfer process, and conversation history handoff
- **Accuracy & Audit Schedule** — Weekly through annual audit cadences covering chat logs, bias detection, and hallucination audits
- **KPIs & Success Metrics** — Seven measurable targets including CSAT, resolution rate, and zero data privacy incidents

## Regulations & Frameworks Referenced

GDPR · CCPA · FTC Guidelines · Utah AI Policy Act · California SB 243

## KPI Targets

| KPI | Target |
|-----|--------|
| Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT) | ≥ 85% |
| First Contact Resolution Rate | ≥ 75% |
| Escalation Rate | ≤ 20% |
| Response Accuracy Rate | ≥ 90% |
| Average Response Time | < 3 seconds |
| Compliance Audit Pass Rate | 100% |
| Data Privacy Incidents | 0 |

## Business Context

This document demonstrates a core AI Analyst competency: defining the governance, safety, and compliance requirements that must be embedded into every AI initiative before development begins. It translates regulatory requirements into actionable specifications for development and architecture teams.
